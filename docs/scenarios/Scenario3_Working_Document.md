**The working document for Scenario 3.** As Scenario 1, with 10% rooftop, 10% parking canopy, and
80% remaining utility-scale of which 90% agrivoltaic; FERC 2222 participation and hourly retail
rates.

`Internal_Debugging_Log.md`; open problems live in GitHub issues. Cross-cutting facts that apply to

## 1. Result

**Not started.** Blocked on issue #14 — every Scenario 3 capacity figure depends on the distributed
siting cap, which rests on a per-capita basis that has been discredited and needs re-deriving on
commercial and industrial floor area.

Also the most expensive scenario to run: the distributed segment costs roughly 9x the solve time.

## 3. Agrivoltaics

**`Appendix_Agrivoltaics.md` carries this in full** — land use at 5–7 acres/MW on the SEIA basis,
regional practice, crop and livestock compatibility, lease income, and the evidence reviewed.

**Agrivoltaic siting should apply to every scenario, not this one alone.** Carrying it here only
gave Scenario 3 a benefit stream the others were denied by construction. It is a siting choice, and
Scenario 1's 156,737 MW has to go somewhere.

---

## 4. DER owner economics

**Blocked** on issues #5 and #15 — DER foresight and the revenue stack.

Net metering is a compensation mechanism, not a requirement, and appears nowhere in the Code's
capacity provisions. It determines what a distributed owner is paid for exports, not how much
distributed capacity must exist — so it belongs here rather than in the statutory carve-out.

---

## 5. Transmission deferral

**The secondary objective of the whole study**: generating clean firmed energy in and around urban
and suburban areas to reduce the need for new high-voltage transmission.

Needs issue #4, nodal prices. And note that `Common_Reference.md` section 1.4 records that avoided
losses from distributed generation are credited nowhere in the model — worth roughly 9% of the
energy displaced, and relevant here.
