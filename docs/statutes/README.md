# Virginia Code — Statutory Basis

Verbatim text of the Virginia Code sections underlying this analysis. Public domain.
Included so reviewers can check compliance claims against primary text rather than
against this project's characterization of it.

| File | Section | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| `56-585.5.md` | Generation of electricity from renewable and zero carbon sources | The RPS itself. Compliance base definition, percentage schedule, solar/storage minimums, ACEB provisions, deficiency payments |
| `56-585.1-4.md` | Development of solar, wind, and storage capacity | Public-interest declarations; third-party ownership requirements (25% solar, 35% storage) |
| `45.2-1706.1.md` | Commonwealth Clean Energy Policy | Policy goals including distributed energy resources; note the 2040 carbon-free aspiration vs the 2045 statutory endpoint |
| `45.2-1710.md` | Development of the Virginia Energy Plan | The twelve required Plan elements — effectively a specification for submissions |
| `45.2-1701.md` | Division of Renewable Energy and Energy Efficiency | Establishes the Division and its consultative process |
| `45.2-1702.md` | Definitions (energy performance contracting) | ESPC mechanism — an existing delivery pathway for DSM measures |

**Currency:** § 56-585.5 as uploaded carries amendments through 2026 (cc. 43, 512, 645, 646,
694, 695, 733, 734), including HB 895 / SB 448. § 56-585.1:4 carries amendments only through
2023. Verify currency at law.lis.virginia.gov before citing in a filing.

**Note on conditional provisions:** § 56-585.5(E)(3) and (E)(4) — the long-duration storage
targets — are marked "Effective pursuant to Acts 2026, cc. 694, 695, cl. 4," and are contingent
on an SCC determination of technical viability and achievability, with a final order due no
later than March 1, 2031.
